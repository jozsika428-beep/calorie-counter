About & credits

Free, private calorie & macro counter. No tracking, no ads, no account — all data stays in this browser.

Based on data from NEVO online version 2025/9.0, RIVM, Bilthoven (and other data sources)

  • NEVO — Dutch Food Composition Database, RIVM.
  • USDA FoodData Central — nutrient data and serving sizes (public domain).
  • Photo recognition: Google AIY vision classifier food_V1 (MobileNet V1, Apache License 2.0), converted to ONNX; runs on-device with ONNX Runtime Web (MIT License). Photos never leave your device.
  • Open Food Facts — online product search & barcode lookup; data available under the Open Database License (ODbL).
  • Fonts (bundled, SIL Open Font License 1.1): Inter, Poppins, Nunito, Roboto, Montserrat, Lora, Playfair Display, Comic Neue and JetBrains Mono — © their respective project authors (see fonts/licenses/).

Nutrient values are shown as provided by the source (per 100 g or 100 ml); they are only rounded for display. Tap “Details & source” when adding a food to see its exact values and origin.

Visual style inspired by the Cal AI app. This is an independent free project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Cal AI, and it uses none of its name, logo or assets.
